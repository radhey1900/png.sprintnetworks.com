The problem

PNG IT is hard.
You shouldn't have to fight it every day.

Most WAN setups in PNG were built for a different country, a different network, and a different decade. Here's what that looks like on the ground.

🌐

National links feel like a lottery

Telikom CS², PPC-1, PNG LNG — they exist, but you don't control the path. A cut in one cable and half your branches go dark while the other half slow to a crawl. You find out when users complain.

📡

Remote sites run on prayer and satellite

Mine sites, aid posts, rural offices — Starlink where licensed, O3b backhaul, microwave, or nothing at all. Each site is a one-off. No consistency, no central visibility, no real SLA.

🛡️

Security is a branch office afterthought

Every branch rolls its own firewall rules, every ISP gets full inbound, and your central team has no idea what's actually exposed until something happens. Cloud apps get the same treatment as internal servers.

🔧

When something breaks, you find out from users

No central monitoring. No alerting. No runbook. A link flaps in Lae, a site loses its satellite session, a firewall rule changes — and your NOC finds out when the CFO calls.

What Sprint fixes

One platform. Every site. Visible and managed.

Versa Networks SD-WAN and SASE gives you a single fabric across every branch in PNG — fixed, satellite, mobile, whatever the link is. Sprint deploys it, monitors it, and supports it.
