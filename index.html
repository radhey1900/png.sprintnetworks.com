Our approach

Most IT in PNG is cobbled together — a link here, a firewall there, a satellite dish because the site is remote. Sprint starts from the outcomes you need and designs the infrastructure to deliver them, then tests it until it holds.

Existing links, traffic patterns, application needs, failure history, security gaps. We don't guess what your branches need — we find out.

What this looks like for you

Minimum outage. Maximum security. Visible everywhere.

This is the outcome we design for. Most customers don't think about their WAN until something goes wrong — we build it so going wrong is rare, short, and handled before it becomes your problem.

Outcome How Sprint delivers it What you see
Outages are rare Redundant paths, automatic failover, per-application routing — designed in from day one, not bolted on after a crisis. A cable cut or ISP issue doesn't become a branch-down event
When something does go wrong, it's short Failover is automatic. Monitoring catches it. Sprint sees it before users report it. Recovery is designed in, not improvised. Minutes, not hours. And often no one notices
Security is everywhere, not just at HQ SASE means every branch gets the same cloud-grade security policy. Zero-trust access to your apps. No branch is its own weakest link. One security posture across the whole network
You can see what's happening Central monitoring and alerting. Per-site, per-link, per-application visibility. Sprint runs the NOC view — you don't have to. Dashboards, alerts, regular reviews — not a black box
It keeps working after go-live Sprint manages and supports — policy reviews, change control, fault response. The design we built is maintained, not abandoned. A network that doesn't silently decay over 18 months
